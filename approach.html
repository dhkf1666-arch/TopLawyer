Our Approach

Making the complicated
clear enough to decide.

We don't substitute a stack of clauses for judgment. We use plain commercial language to help clients make executable decisions under uncertainty.

“We believe the best legal advice doesn't make risk sound more complicated — it makes a complicated situation clear enough for a client to decide.” — Nathaniel Ashford, Founding Partner
01

One partner, start to finish

Every matter is led personally by a senior partner, never simply attached to one — so accountability is never diluted across a team.

02

Commercial language, not legal jargon

Advice should be understood by the boardroom before it's tested by a judge — understanding comes first, wording follows.

03

Response times you can count on

An initial view within 24 hours on material matters; urgent matters, any hour — clients shouldn't carry extra risk while they wait.

04

Transparent fee structures

Clear engagement terms and budget ranges before work begins, with progress shared proactively — never a surprise after the fact.

Our History

Twenty-eight years, a few defining moments

1998

The firm is founded

Nathaniel Ashford founds the practice in London, initially focused on corporate and contract work.

2006

A dedicated disputes team is formed

The firm builds a standalone litigation and arbitration practice, taking on its first cross-border arbitration matters.

2013

New York and Hong Kong offices open

The firm's reach extends across the Atlantic and into Asia, serving clients across the world's major industrial corridors.

2019

IP becomes a standalone practice

Responding to demand from technology clients, intellectual property is spun out as its own dedicated practice line.

2024

Singapore, Dubai and Geneva

The firm completes its footprint across the major financial centres serving clients' global ambitions.
